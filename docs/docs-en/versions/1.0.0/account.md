# Account and Feedback

## Registration

On the sign-in page, choose the registration option. You can register by email or phone. Email registration requires application review and email activation. A phone number supplied during email registration cannot be used to sign in until you verify it by SMS in your account settings.

For phone registration, enter a password, basic information, and your intended use. Email is optional. Once SMS verification succeeds, you can sign in without application review.

If the activation email does not arrive, check your spam folder or contact the platform administrator.

## Signing in and account settings

Sign in with your registered email address or verified phone number and the same account password. The optional contact email supplied during phone registration can also be used to sign in. If you forget your password, request a reset link at your configured email address or an SMS code at your verified phone number.

Your account appears at the top right after sign-in. Open the account settings through the additional-actions menu to change your password, activate an existing phone number by SMS, add or replace a phone number, or add or replace your sign-in email using email verification. A verified phone number works with your existing password. Sign in again after changing your password. Credentials for ZhisiTi accounts are managed by ZhisiTi.

## Run allowance

The account area shows your remaining simulation runs for the day. If the allowance is insufficient, the platform displays a message before starting the run. Avoid submitting the same run repeatedly.

## Mailbox and simulation completion notifications

Click **Mailbox** at the top of the page to view notifications within the platform. The list on the right shows each notification's title, sender, timestamp, and read status. Select a notification to read its content on the left. Use the refresh button at the top right to update the list.

A simulation completion notification shows the scenario and the time the run was started. Click **View Results** in the notification to open the corresponding simulation results without rerunning it.

## Contact and feedback

Use the contact-author button at the top of the page to submit feedback. Categories include bug reports, author contact, collaboration, product suggestions, account issues, and other requests.

Describe the affected page, the steps you took, and any messages displayed. Attach a screenshot when it helps explain an interface issue. Do not include passwords, API keys, or other sensitive information.
